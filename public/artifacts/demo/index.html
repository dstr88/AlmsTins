This page runs the real thing in miniature: a domain-separated SHA-256 Merkle tree over the records, a canonical manifest, and a digest an anchor pins to a time it doesn't control. Signature uses your browser's Ed25519 where available. In production, Almstins signs the manifest with its own Ed25519 key and anchors the digest to Bitcoin via OpenTimestamps — so "verified since" is provable against a public chain, not against our word. Change the date and the anchored digest stops matching. That is the whole idea: the plumbing hides, the proof never does.
