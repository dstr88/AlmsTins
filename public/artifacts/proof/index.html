What this proof claims

Scope is explicit. This proves control of the address only. It is not a claim of legal ownership of any underlying asset, and not a statement that the address is safe to transact with.

What "Verified" means here — and what it never claims

  • Unaltered and dated
    An unaltered proof in the Almstins format, signed with a TEST key and submitted for a Bitcoin timestamp. This part is fully checked below. It vouches for the document, and, once Bitcoin confirms the receipt, for when it existed.
  • Control of the address — not who holds it
    It attests that whoever controls this address proved it with an action only the key-holder can take. When the claim is an on-chain action, the record can carry a transaction hash so you pull it off the chain yourself — the hash and the status are optional, since not every claim is on-chain. In this demo the self-send entries are illustrative.
  • Never a person
    Almstins does not link an address to a legal identity. It proves control, never ownership by a named party. That refusal is the design, not a gap.
