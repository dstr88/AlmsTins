Verify · Milestone Desk demo

The money moves when the roof is finished. Who says the roof is finished?

Tranche finance releases money against milestones, and every release rests on somebody's word that the work is real. This walks one project end to end: a GH₵800,000 classroom block in Kumasi, a sealed milestone schedule, and an independent inspector answering a single-use link from the site. The answer is signed, bound to the evidence, and timestamped into Bitcoin — a trail a lender can rely on and a stranger can check.

This is a demo of the mechanism, not the production desk. The cryptography is real — the schedule and the attestation are signed with Ed25519 in your browser, and the answer can be timestamped into Bitcoin through the same anchor the live desk uses. Nothing here moves money. Nothing on the live desk moves money either: it is the evidence layer above the rails, never the rails.

    3The trail — what the lender relies on

      Recording a release writes evidence that money moved — by whom, when, against which attested milestone. Almstins never touches the money itself. The release happens on the bank's own rails; the record is what survives.

      Why this is a trail and not a report

      Monitoring produces reports. Verification produces collateral. A report is somebody's summary, editable until the moment it is needed. Every entry here is signed the instant it is made, bound to the exact schedule and the exact evidence files by digest, and stamped into a chain nobody operates. Swap a photo, reword a milestone, backdate a finding — and the arithmetic breaks in public.

      The inspector needs nothing but the link. No account, no app, no relationship with Almstins. The single-use token is the capability: reading is free and unlimited, answering spends it. That is deliberate — the person standing on the site with muddy boots should face the least friction in the whole system.

      And the direction of power stays fixed. The borrower proves their own work. The inspector signs their own words. The banker records their own release. Nobody here is watched; everybody here is answerable, each for exactly the thing they put their name to. That distinction is the whole product.
